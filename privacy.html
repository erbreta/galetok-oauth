Galetok Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026.

What Galetok accesses

Galetok uses Android's media and folder permissions to show the photos, videos and files you can access on your device. You can use the gallery without connecting a cloud account.

If you connect Google Drive, Galetok uses your Google account email and display name to identify the connection. It reads Drive storage usage, folder and file metadata, and file contents as needed to sync the albums you pair. You can separately choose to grant full Drive access to pair existing folders.

How Galetok uses and stores this data

Galetok communicates directly with Google Drive to list folders, upload and download selected album files, reconcile changes and move synced deletions to recoverable Trash. Your paired media is stored in your own Drive account and on your device. Galetok keeps account details, album mappings, sync history and recovery information on the device. Google Play services manages authorization for the current Android Drive connection.

Galetok uses Google data for the Drive features you enable. It does not sell that data, send it to a Galetok-operated server, use it for advertising or share it with advertisers.

Galetok's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

Unpair an album or remove the connected Google account in Sync to stop syncing. You can also revoke Galetok's access in your Google Account. Removing the connection does not erase copies already stored in Drive or on your device. You can manage those copies separately. Clearing Galetok's app storage in Android removes its app settings and sync database.

For privacy questions, use the support contact on Galetok's Google consent screen.
