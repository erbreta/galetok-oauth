Galetok

Galetok is an Android gallery and file manager for browsing and organizing photos and videos stored on your device.

Google Drive sync

Drive sync is optional. Open Sync, connect your Google account and choose the albums you want to sync. Basic access covers the Drive files Galetok creates or is authorized to use. Browsing existing Drive folders requires a separate permission.

Galetok shows sync activity and provides recoverable Trash for supported synced deletions.
